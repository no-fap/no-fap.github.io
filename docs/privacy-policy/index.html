Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

Welcome to NoFap. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our application, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Authentication Data: When you log in using Google Sign-In, we collect your basic profile information, including your name, email address (e.g., yashdaive@hotmail.com), and profile picture.
  • App Usage Data: We collect self-reported daily logs, streak statistics, and in-app milestone achievements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly to provide and improve the service. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Authenticate your identity securely via Firebase Authentication.
  • Track your personal progress, streaks, and milestones within the application.
  • Enable group accountability features, allowing group members to view shared progress and real-time updates.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties. Your data is solely used for the functionality of the app.

Information regarding your progress and relapses is shared only with the explicit members of your designated accountability groups.

4. Data Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Auth infrastructures, which employ industry-standard encryption and security protocols to protect against unauthorized access.

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the developer at:
